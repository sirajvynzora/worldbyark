Meet the Founder

Where One Journey Became Thousands of Memories.

Born and raised in the beautiful state of Kerala, India, Anjali Rose always dreamed of exploring the world beyond the familiar. While she loved discovering new places through books, photographs, and stories, international travel felt like a distant dream.

In 2013, that dream became reality when she moved to Germany to pursue her MBA. It was her first time living away from home and her first opportunity to experience a completely different world. Every street, culture, language, and tradition was new, opening her eyes to possibilities she had never imagined.

The journey wasn't easy. As someone naturally shy, she initially found it difficult to speak with people from different countries. She often questioned whether they would understand her, appreciate her culture, or judge her because of where she came from. Living in an unfamiliar country, adapting to a new lifestyle, and stepping outside her comfort zone became some of the biggest challenges of her life.

Meet Our Founder

Anjali Rose

Founder & Travel Designer
From a shy young woman leaving Kerala for the first time to a confident traveller connecting people across cultures.

In 2013, Anjali Rose left everything familiar behind in Kerala, India, and moved to Germany to pursue her MBA. It was her first opportunity to experience a completely different world filled with new cultures, exciting possibilities and unforgettable experiences.

At first, she was shy and hesitant to speak with people from different countries. She wondered whether they would understand her, accept her culture or judge her because of where she came from. But life had a very different journey waiting for her.

Living in Europe opened doors she had never imagined. Every few weeks, she explored a new country or city, discovered different cultures, tasted local cuisines, attended festivals and met people from around the world.

Travel taught her that the world becomes smaller when people understand, respect and connect with one another.
